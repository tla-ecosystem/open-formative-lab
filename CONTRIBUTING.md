# Contributing to OFAL

Thanks for your interest! OFAL is in its planning stage.

- **Ideas and use cases:** open an issue describing how you'd use OFAL or what blocks you today.
- **Code:** contributions are licensed under Apache-2.0. Please keep all dependencies openly licensed (MIT, Apache-2.0, or similar permissive licenses).
- **Data:** shared data must be CC-BY-4.0 compatible and contain no personally identifiable student information.
